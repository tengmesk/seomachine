## Executive Summary

"Custom software development company London" is Conceptdigital's highest-intent core-service keyword: transactional buyers actively shortlisting a vendor. The page-one SERP is dominated by **directories** (Clutch, DesignRush) and **agency landing pages** (One Beyond, TRooTech, Magneto, RSK-BSL) — most are thin sales pages or generic listicles. The opportunity is a genuinely useful, buyer-first pillar guide (pricing transparency, vendor-evaluation framework, London trust signals, real case studies) surrounded by 10 supporting articles that own the cost, comparison, vertical, and process long-tails competitors under-serve.

### Competitive Analysis
- **Clutch / DesignRush (directories)** — Strength: domain authority, structured firm comparison. Gap: no narrative guidance, no opinion, no pricing depth; we can out-teach them.
- **One Beyond / TRooTech / RSK-BSL (agency pages)** — Strength: clear service framing, awards. Gap: pure sales pages, no buyer education, no transparent pricing, weak FAQ/PAA coverage.
- **Listicles ("Top companies in London 2026")** — Strength: match navigational intent. Gap: shallow, often pay-to-play, quickly stale; a maintained, genuinely useful guide outlasts them.

**Validated market facts to cite** (from web research, June 2026):
- 27,000+ software development companies operate in London (market is crowded → buyer-selection guidance is high-value).
- UK cross-functional team rates ≈ £60–£120/hr; London boutiques/enterprise consultancies £120–£160+/hr.
- Typical project bands: simple tool/MVP £8k–£30k; standard business app £30k–£90k; complex platform £70k–£150k; enterprise £150k–£500k+.
- Ongoing maintenance ≈ 15–25% of build cost per year.
