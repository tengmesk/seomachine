## Internal Linking Best Practices

### Priority Levels

**Tier 1 — Link Often** (3–5 links per article):
- The most relevant service page for the article's primary topic
- The most relevant capability page if industry-specific
- The most relevant case study that proves the outcome being discussed
- Contact page in conclusion or CTA section

**Tier 2 — Link Sometimes** (1–2 links per article):
- About page when establishing company credibility
- Adjacent service pages where a natural connection exists
- Secondary case studies for additional social proof

**Tier 3 — Link Rarely**:
- Homepage
- Careers page (only in talent/hiring articles)
- Our Clients hub (only when referencing the portfolio broadly)

### Linking Guidelines

**Do**:
- Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text that reflects what the linked page is about
- Link to the most specific relevant page (a role-specific augmentation page over the general homepage)
- Place a link in the first third of an article when a natural opportunity appears
- Vary anchor text — do not use identical phrasing for the same destination in every article
- Link to case studies when claiming an outcome (e.g. "helped raise funding" → Rex case study)

**Do Not**:
- Use generic anchor text ("click here", "read more", "learn more")
- Link to the homepage when a specific service or capability page exists
- Add more than two links in a single paragraph
- Link to the same page more than once per article
- Force a link that does not genuinely help the reader
