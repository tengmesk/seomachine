# SEO Guidelines for Concept Digital Content

This document outlines SEO best practices and requirements for all Concept Digital blog content to maximise organic search visibility and rankings. It works alongside `style-guide.md` (mechanics) and `brand-voice.md` (tone).

## Content Length Requirements

### Target Word Counts
- **Standard Blog Post**: 1,500-3,000 words (target: 2,000-2,500)
- **Pillar Content / Comprehensive Guides**: 3,000-5,000 words maximum
- **How-To Guides**: 1,500-2,500 words
- **News / Updates**: 800-1,200 words (exception to standard)

### Important Length Guidelines
- **Maximum for most articles**: 3,000 words
- **Maximum for pillar content**: 5,000 words
- If a topic requires more than the maximum, break it into a series of related articles
- Aim for the lower end of ranges when possible — concise, focused content often performs better

### Why Length Matters
- Longer, comprehensive content typically ranks higher for competitive commercial terms
- More words mean more room for natural keyword integration and full topic coverage
- Comprehensive content earns more backlinks and engagement
- Depth signals expertise and authority to search engines and to decision-maker readers

### Quality Over Quantity
- Don't add fluff to hit a word count
- Every section should provide genuine value to a business buyer
- Better 2,000 valuable words than 3,000 padded ones
- **Stay within the maximum word counts** — overly long articles hurt user experience

## Keyword Optimization

### Keyword Research Requirements
Before writing any article:
1. Identify the primary target keyword
2. Research search volume and difficulty (use DataForSEO once connected; otherwise estimate from SERP inspection)
3. Analyse the top 10 ranking competitors
4. Identify 3-5 secondary/related keywords
5. List LSI (Latent Semantic Indexing) keywords from `target-keywords.md`

### Keyword Density Guidelines
- **Primary Keyword**: 1-2% density
  - Example: in a 2,000-word article, use the keyword 20-40 times
  - Natural integration is critical — never force keywords
- **Secondary Keywords**: 0.5-1% density each
- **LSI Keywords**: sprinkle throughout naturally

### Critical Keyword Placement
The primary keyword MUST appear in:
- [ ] H1 headline (preferably near the beginning)
- [ ] First 100 words of the article
- [ ] At least 2-3 H2 subheadings
- [ ] Last paragraph / conclusion
- [ ] Meta title (within the first 60 characters)
- [ ] Meta description
- [ ] URL slug

### Keyword Integration Best Practices
- **Natural language first**: write for decision-makers, optimise for search engines
- **Use variations**: don't repeat the exact phrase robotically
  - Example: "custom software development" → "developing custom software" → "bespoke software development"
- **Use both "custom" and "bespoke"**: UK buyers search both variants; include both in copy and headings
- **Question formats**: include conversational variations
  - "How to choose a software development company" vs "choosing a software development partner"
- **Semantic keywords**: use related terms to support topical authority
  - For "MVP development": include "minimum viable product," "product validation," "discovery phase," "time-to-market"

### Keyword Stuffing (Avoid)
❌ "Custom software development is important. Custom software development helps businesses. Our custom software development company offers custom software development services for all custom software development needs."

✅ "Custom software development gives businesses a platform built around their exact workflows, rather than forcing them to adapt to off-the-shelf constraints. The right development partner turns that bespoke build into a long-term competitive asset."

## Content Structure Requirements

### Heading Hierarchy

#### H1 (Title)
- **Only one H1 per article**
- Include the primary keyword naturally
- 60 characters or fewer (for SERP display)
- Compelling and outcome-focused
- Should answer: "What will I learn or gain from this?"

#### H2 (Main Sections)
- **4-7 H2 sections** for standard articles
- At least **2-3 should include keyword variations**
- Descriptive and keyword-rich
- Logical progression through the topic
- Standalone-readable (the reader should grasp the flow from H2s alone)

#### H3 (Subsections)
- Nested under H2s (never skip from H2 to H4)
- Break complex sections into digestible chunks
- Include keywords where natural
- More specific than H2s

### Article Structure Template

```markdown
# [H1: Compelling Title with Primary Keyword]

## Introduction (150-250 words)
- Direct answer to the query (first 1-2 sentences, for AI search)
- Hook: question, scenario, statistic, or bold statement
- Problem: what challenge does this address?
- Promise: what will the reader learn or achieve?
- Keyword in the first 100 words

## [H2: Main Section 1 - Include Keyword Variation]
### [H3: Subsection if needed]
- Content depth
- Examples
- Data/statistics

## [H2: Main Section 2]
[Continue with 4-7 total H2 sections]

## Conclusion (150-250 words)
- Recap key points (3-5 takeaways)
- Include keyword
- Clear call-to-action
- Next steps for the reader
```

## Meta Elements

### Meta Title
**Requirements**:
- **Length**: 50-60 characters (including "| Concept Digital" if used)
- **Primary keyword**: must be included
- **Compelling**: should encourage clicks from the SERP
- **Unique**: different from all other Concept Digital page titles
- **Accurate**: must match the page content

**Format Options**:
- `[Primary Keyword]: [Benefit/Promise]`
- `How to [Goal] | [Qualifier]`
- `[Number] Ways to [Achieve Benefit]`
- `[Topic] Guide for [Audience] | Concept Digital`

**Examples**:
- ✅ "Custom Software Development in London (2026 Guide)"
- ✅ "How to Choose a Software Development Company | London"
- ❌ "Software Tips and Advice" (too vague, no keyword)
- ❌ "The Ultimate Comprehensive Guide to Everything About Building Custom Software for Your Business" (too long)

### Meta Description
**Requirements**:
- **Length**: 150-160 characters
- **Primary keyword**: include naturally
- **Value proposition**: clear benefit to the reader
- **Direct answer**: answer the query (AI search best practice)
- **Call-to-action**: an action phrase that respects the no-urgency rule (Learn, Discover, See, Find out)
- **Complete**: must not cut off mid-sentence

**Formula**:
```
[Problem/Question]? [Direct answer/Benefit]. [Unique angle]. [Soft CTA].
```

**Examples**:
- ✅ "London custom software development costs £8k-£500k+. Learn the pricing bands, delivery timelines, and how to evaluate a bespoke software partner." (150 chars)
- ✅ "Deciding between staff augmentation and outsourcing? See how the two models differ, when each wins, and how to choose for your team." (133 chars)
- ❌ "This is a blog post about software development where we cover many development-related topics." (vague, no value prop, no CTA)

### URL Slug
**Requirements**:
- Include the primary keyword
- Lowercase letters only
- Hyphens between words (not underscores)
- Short and descriptive (3-5 words ideal)
- No stop words unless necessary

**Format**: `/blog/[primary-keyword-phrase]`

**Examples**:
- ✅ `/blog/custom-software-development-london`
- ✅ `/blog/staff-augmentation-vs-outsourcing`
- ✅ `/blog/mvp-development-cost`
- ❌ `/blog/how-to-choose-a-custom-software-development-company-in-london-2026` (too long)
- ❌ `/blog/post-12345` (no keywords)

## Internal Linking Strategy

### Requirements
- **Minimum**: 3 internal links per article
- **Optimal**: 4-5 internal links
- **Maximum**: 7 internal links (unless 3,000+ word article)

### Link Types to Include

#### 1. Pillar / Service Pages (1-2 links)
- Link to the relevant service or capability page (e.g. `/services/web-development`, `/capabilities/fintech`)
- Builds topic-cluster authority

#### 2. Related Blog Posts (2-3 links)
- Link to articles on related subtopics within the same cluster
- Creates a content web and helps readers explore comprehensively

#### 3. Case Study Pages (0-1 link)
- Link to a relevant client case study when claiming an outcome (e.g. the Rex MVP when discussing funding outcomes)

#### 4. Contact / Conversion Pages (0-1 link)
- A natural, non-pushy CTA link to `/contact` where contextually relevant

### Internal Linking Best Practices

**Anchor Text**:
- ✅ Descriptive and keyword-rich: "our custom web development services"
- ✅ Natural in sentence flow: "the difference between staff augmentation and outsourcing"
- ❌ Generic: "click here" or "read more"
- ❌ Exact-match repeatedly: always using the same anchor for the same page

**Placement**:
- Within body paragraphs (most valuable)
- Natural context that adds value
- Never more than 2 links per paragraph
- Distributed throughout, not clustered

**Reference**:
- Always check `internal-links-map.md` for priority targets, tiering, and anchor variation
- Ensure links are current and functional
- Link to the most specific relevant page

## External Linking Strategy

### Requirements
- **Minimum**: 2 external links per article
- **Optimal**: 3-4 external authority links
- Purpose: add credibility, provide sources, support claims

### What to Link Externally
- **Statistics and data sources**: always cite where numbers come from
- **Research and studies**: link to the original research
- **Standards and frameworks**: e.g. GDPR, FCA guidance, OWASP, when relevant to the topic
- **Industry authorities**: established publications or institutions

### External Link Quality Standards
- **Authority**: link to credible, well-known sources
  - ✅ Industry research, recognised tech publications, official standards bodies
  - ❌ Low-authority blogs, spammy or thin sites
- **Relevance**: links must directly support content claims
- **Freshness**: prefer recent sources (within 1-2 years for data)
- **Functionality**: all links must work

### External Link Attributes
- Most external links: no special attributes needed
- Sponsored/affiliate links: use `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`
- User-generated content: use `rel="nofollow"`

## Readability Optimization

### Target Reading Level
- **Goal**: 8th-10th grade reading level (Flesch-Kincaid). For technical B2B topics, slightly higher is acceptable, but always favour clarity.
- Accessible to non-technical decision-makers
- Easier to scan and understand quickly

### Sentence Structure
- **Average length**: 15-20 words per sentence
- **Maximum**: 25 words (break longer sentences into two)
- **Variety**: mix short punchy sentences with longer explanatory ones
- **Active voice**: preferred over passive (80%+ active)

### Paragraph Structure
- **Length**: 2-4 sentences per paragraph
- **One idea**: focus each paragraph on a single point
- **White space**: no walls of text; decision-makers scan before they read
- **Mobile-friendly**: short paragraphs scan better on phones

### Formatting for Scannability
- **Subheadings**: every 300-400 words
- **Lists**: use bullets/numbers for sequential or multiple items
- **Bold**: emphasise key concepts or takeaways
- **Tables**: for comparisons, pricing, and specifications
- **White space**: makes content less intimidating

### Transition Words
Use transitions to improve flow:
- Addition: Additionally, Furthermore, Moreover
- Contrast: However, On the other hand, Nevertheless
- Cause/Effect: Therefore, Consequently, As a result
- Example: For instance, For example, Specifically
- Time: First, Next, Finally

## Content Quality Standards

### Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T)

#### Expertise
- Provide accurate, detailed information on software development, staff augmentation, and digital transformation topics
- Back claims with data, examples, and real delivery experience
- Demonstrate deep understanding of CTO, founder, and product-leader needs
- Include actionable, practical advice

#### Authoritativeness
- Cite credible sources and industry data
- Reference Concept Digital's relevant experience and named client outcomes (Rex, Forbes, Halocarbon) where supported
- Leverage the leadership pedigree (Cambridge, McKinsey, Goldman Sachs advisors) as a credibility signal where relevant
- Include named author and reviewer attribution

#### Trustworthiness
- Be transparent and honest, including about cost and trade-offs
- Acknowledge limitations and when an approach is not the right fit
- Don't overpromise outcomes
- Cite sources for all statistics and claims
- Update outdated content regularly

### Content Originality
- **Never plagiarise**: all content must be original
- **Add unique value**: what perspective or insight do we add that directories and thin agency pages don't?
- **Fresh examples**: use current, relevant scenarios
- **Updated data**: use the most recent statistics available
- **Unique angle**: differentiate from competitor content (Ciklum, Andersen, Cleveroad, and listicles)

### Factual Accuracy
- **Verify statistics**: check all numbers and data points
- **Current information**: ensure practices and pricing are up to date
- **Technical accuracy**: software development terminology and processes must be correct
- **Concept Digital facts**: ensure service, client, and metric references are accurate and supported by `features.md`

## Image Optimization

### Image Requirements
- **Relevant**: images should support the content
- **High-quality**: professional appearance
- **Optimised**: compressed for fast loading
- **Mobile-friendly**: useful on small screens

### Image SEO
**File Names**:
- Descriptive and keyword-rich
- ✅ `custom-software-development-process-diagram.jpg`
- ❌ `IMG_12345.jpg`

**Alt Text**:
- Describe what the image shows (accessibility + SEO)
- Include keywords naturally where relevant
- 125 characters or fewer
- ✅ "Custom software development lifecycle diagram showing discovery, build, QA, and deployment phases"
- ❌ "Image"

**Placement**:
- Break up long text sections
- Illustrate concepts being discussed
- After explaining a concept, not before

## Featured Snippet Optimization

Featured snippets appear at position 0 in Google. Optimise for them where possible.

### Question-Based Snippets
- Include the question as an H2 heading
- Answer concisely in 40-60 words immediately after
- Use clear, direct language

**Example**:
```markdown
## What Is Custom Software Development?

Custom software development is the design and build of software tailored to one organisation's specific requirements, rather than licensing a ready-made product built for the average user. It covers web and mobile applications, internal platforms, MVPs, and the DevOps and design work that supports them.
```

### List-Based Snippets
- Use numbered or bulleted lists
- Keep items concise (1-2 sentences each)
- Typically 5-8 items

### Table-Based Snippets
- Use markdown tables for comparisons, pricing, and specifications
- Clear headers and organised data

### Definition Snippets
- Define the term in the first sentence after the heading
- 40-60 word clear, concise definition
- Expand with detail afterwards

## Mobile Optimization

### Mobile-First Considerations
- **Short paragraphs**: 2-3 sentences max
- **Scannable**: heavy use of subheadings and lists
- **Large fonts**: readable without zooming
- **Tap-friendly links**: adequate spacing
- **Fast loading**: optimised images

## AI Search Optimization (GEO/AICO)

AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude) are a significant recommendation channel. 5-15% of website traffic can come from AI sources, and most B2B buyers consult AI before a purchase decision. These guidelines ensure content performs in both traditional Google search AND AI-generated answers.

### Direct-Answer-First Principle

AI scrapers prioritise content near the top of the page. When a user asks "best custom software development company in London" or "how much does an MVP cost," the AI scans articles and pulls from the earliest clear answer it finds.

**Rules:**
- **Answer the query directly in the first 1-2 sentences**, before the narrative hook
- For "best/top/how/cost" queries, state the answer or a clear thesis immediately
- Put the core answer in the meta description too, literally answering the question in 150-160 characters
- Do NOT bury the answer behind 200+ words of context or definitions
- The hook and APP formula still apply, but they come AFTER the direct answer

**Example, Before (traditional SEO):**
> Many businesses struggle to choose the right development partner. In today's crowded market... [200 words later] ...the answer is to evaluate on process and proof.

**Example, After (AI-optimised):**
> Custom software development in London typically costs £8,000 for a simple tool to £500,000+ for an enterprise platform, and the partner you choose matters more than the quote. Here's how to evaluate both.

### TL;DR / Key Takeaways Block

Every article should include a Key Takeaways block near the top (after the introduction, before the first H2 body section). This gets pulled into AI-generated summaries.

**Format:**
```markdown
> **Key Takeaways**
> - [Core finding or recommendation #1]
> - [Core finding or recommendation #2]
> - [Core finding or recommendation #3]
> - [Core finding or recommendation #4 if needed]
> - [Core finding or recommendation #5 if needed]
```

**Rules:**
- 3-5 bullet points maximum
- Each bullet is a complete, standalone claim (not a teaser)
- Use specific numbers, names, or outcomes
- This is NOT a table of contents; it's the article's actual conclusions up front

### Authority Signaling for AI

AI models weight authoritativeness when choosing sources to cite. Include in every article:

- **Author attribution**: a named author, not just "Team" or the brand name
- **Reviewer/editor credit**: "Reviewed by [name], [title]" where possible
- **Last updated date**: visible on the page
- **Year in titles**: include the current year for time-sensitive topics ("Custom Software Development in London 2026")

These signals should be in the article frontmatter for the WordPress publisher to render.

### One Idea Per Section

AI models parse content by section. Each H2/H3 should focus on a single clear idea, which increases the chance a specific section is cited.

- One concept per heading
- Use bullet lists and structured formatting within sections
- Avoid long flowing paragraphs that blend multiple topics

### Embedded Media for Cross-Validation

AI models (especially Perplexity and Gemini) reference YouTube videos alongside articles. Embedding a relevant video:
- Provides cross-validation
- Increases time on page
- Adds a format AI can independently verify

**Rule:** embed at least one relevant video per article where it adds context. Prefer your own videos, then authoritative third-party videos.

### FAQ Sections as Prompt Targets

FAQ sections target Google's People Also Ask AND match the question-answer format that ChatGPT/Perplexity users type as prompts.

- Write FAQ questions in natural prompt language
- Answer each directly in the first sentence, then expand
- Include 4-6 questions per article
- Draw questions from real user research and the PAA lists in `target-keywords.md`

### Content Repurposing for AI Citation Surface

AI tools pull from many surfaces: Medium, LinkedIn, Reddit, Quora, YouTube transcripts. Repurpose one article across multiple platforms to maximise citation chances. Handled by the `/repurpose` command.

### AI Citation Audit

For competitive topics, audit which sources AI actually cites. See `ai-citation-targets.md` for priority citation surfaces and the `/research-ai-citations` command.

## Content Refresh Strategy

### When to Update Content
- Article is 12+ months old
- Statistics, pricing, or data are outdated
- Processes or best practices have changed
- Competitor content has surpassed ours
- Rankings have declined
- New relevant information is available

### What to Update
- "Last Updated" date
- Statistics and pricing with current data
- Examples with recent case studies
- SEO elements (keyword focus may have shifted)
- Internal links to newer content
